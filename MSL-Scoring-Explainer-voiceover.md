## Recording notes

- Read numbers as: "one forty-six point six seven," "two thirty-one point six seven," "two point three two," "seventy-three point three percent."
- Say "I-P-R" as letters and "RANDA" as a word.
- The on-screen captions stay up the whole time, so the narration doesn't need to match them word for word. It's written to add to them, not repeat them.
- The 1:23 scene's caption ("Scored against 0–100, hitting a goal of 85 would earn 85%, not the intended 50%") is left to the screen. If you'd rather say it, add after "or 146.67": "Scored against zero to 100, hitting a Goal of 85 would earn 85 percent instead of the intended half."

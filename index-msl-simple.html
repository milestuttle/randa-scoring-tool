About This Tool

How to Use

  1. IPR is pre-configured — Measure 1 (Instructional Program Review) defaults to 10% weight. You can adjust this if needed.
  2. Add additional measures — Add up to 3 more measures (4 total).
  3. Enter details — For each measure, provide a name, weight, and the min/max/actual scores.
  4. Balance Weights — Total weight must equal exactly 30%.

Scoring Method

Each measure's score is normalized within its range and scaled to 300 points:

Scaled = ((Actual − Min) / (Max − Min)) × 300

Weighted = Scaled × (Weight / 30)

The final score is the sum of all weighted contributions.
